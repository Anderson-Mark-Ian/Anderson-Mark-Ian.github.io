Animated Musical Feature — In Development

Bloom

A girl who feels invisible to her busy father meets a boy who was once a tree — and must prove the impossible before the forest, and the boy, are lost forever.

A fully-developed original property: complete story, character, and world bibles, a full first-draft screenplay, and a 9-track original soundtrack, several songs already recorded as demos. Thea is written as autistic, drawn from my own daughter; William and her father are ADHD-coded, drawn from my own son and myself.

About

I'm a writer, lyricist, and creator based in Barnoldswick, Lancashire. My work spans poetry, songwriting, and emotionally driven storytelling, with a focus on neurodivergent characters and honest family dynamics — written from the inside, not as an outside observer.

Bloom, an animated musical feature, is where most of my energy sits right now: every lyric in its soundtrack is mine, several songs are already recorded as finished demos, and the concept art for every major character was developed under my direction.

Alongside it: Ember Oracle, a musical project available on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music; A Unified Theory, a non-fiction book published on Amazon and Kindle; and a contribution to the collaborative poetry anthology Poetry for the Peers.

Currently developing Bloom full-time, and actively open to collaborators, representation, and production partners.
